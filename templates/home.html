Why it works

Built to get you back to the book

Most reading apps just store your files. Chapters gives you a reason to open one every day.

XP & levels

Every minute you read and every page you turn adds XP. Watching your level climb makes finishing "just one more chapter" an easy call.

Reading streaks

A visible streak turns reading into a daily habit instead of something you mean to get to. Miss a day and there's a freeze to protect it.

Daily quests

Short, specific goals — "turn 15 pages," "read 20 minutes" — make a long book feel like a series of five-minute wins instead of one big task.

Leaderboard

See how your reading stacks up against everyone else on Chapters. A little friendly competition is often all it takes to open the app again.
